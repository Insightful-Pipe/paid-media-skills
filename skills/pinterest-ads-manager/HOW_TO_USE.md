# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "pinterest-ads-manager" skill. Review my Pinterest Ads account and tell me what to fix before the next campaign.

## What you need

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude, with your Pinterest Ads account added.** The skill pulls the data itself; you don't need to export anything.
- **Pinterest Pages connected too (optional).** It's the only way the skill can read your ad pins' titles, descriptions and video sizes. Without it, those checks are marked unknown and the copy writer asks you to paste your current title.
- **Your target cost per outbound click or cost per result,** if you have one. Without it, the skill uses your account's own figures.
- **A few words about what you sell and one or two Pinterest interests** (for example home_decor, food_and_drinks or womens_fashion) for the seasonal calendar.

## Example requests

**Full review**
Hey Claude, I just added the "pinterest-ads-manager" skill. Review my Pinterest Ads account for the last 30 days. My target cost per outbound click is $0.80.

**Tag health**
Hey Claude, I just added the "pinterest-ads-manager" skill. Check whether my Pinterest tag is firing and whether conversions are reaching my campaigns.

**Formats and targeting**
Hey Claude, I just added the "pinterest-ads-manager" skill. Compare my static, video and carousel pins, and tell me whether keyword or interest targeting is cheaper per outbound click.

**Seasonal plan**
Hey Claude, I just added the "pinterest-ads-manager" skill. Build a launch calendar from Pinterest Trends for home decor and holiday gifts.

**New copy and a paused draft**
Hey Claude, I just added the "pinterest-ads-manager" skill. Rewrite my best ad's title and description, then draft a paused campaign, ad group, keywords and ad for the next peak. Show me the payloads first.

## What you get

1. **A scorecard for seven areas:** delivery and budget pacing, the Pinterest tag, the cost per outbound click trend, creative formats and pin specs, keyword vs interest targeting, actalike and retargeting audiences, and catalog product groups. Each gets pass, warn or fail with the numbers behind it, or says plainly why it couldn't be graded.
2. **A grade out of 100** with the math shown, or "not graded" when most areas lack data.
3. **The top fixes,** ranked by the spend they affect, and the client summary in three sentences.
4. **A seasonal calendar:** when your topics peak on Pinterest and the week to launch before each peak.
5. **New ad titles and descriptions** that fit Pinterest's limits, with what shows in the feed.
6. **Drafted payloads** for a paused campaign, ad group, keywords and ad, each marked "not run" until you say yes.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact payload, it can:
- create campaigns, ad groups and ads (the InsightfulPipe connection creates them paused; you switch them on in Ads Manager)
- add, archive or re-bid keywords in a paused ad group, such as a draft it made
- pause ads that meet its pause rule, or a campaign or ad group you name
- create an image pin for new copy through Pinterest Pages (a new pin goes live on your board straight away)

It never switches a campaign, ad group or ad on, never deletes, and leaves budget, bid and targeting edits on anything that's running to you, keywords included.

## Tips

- Pinterest's analytics reach back 90 days. For older history the skill needs Pinterest's async report and asks before running it.
- Run the review monthly, and the seasonal calendar a quarter ahead of your busy season.
- If you have several ad accounts, say which one to review.
