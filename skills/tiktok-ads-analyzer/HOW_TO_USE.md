# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "tiktok-ads-analyzer" skill. Analyze my TikTok ads: which creatives and audiences work, and what should I change?

## What you need

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude, with your TikTok Ads account added.** The skill pulls campaigns, ad groups, ads and reports itself; you don't need to export anything.
- **A target cost per result,** if you have one. Without it, the skill compares each ad group with your own cost per result for the same goal.
- **Hook or format tags,** if you want a hook-type analysis. Put the type in the ad name (for example "question hook" or "UGC demo"), or tell Claude which ad is which.

**No TikTok account connected yet?** Paste a TikTok Ads Manager export with spend, impressions, clicks, results, 2-second and 6-second views, 100% views and likes, comments and shares per ad. The skill runs the same funnel math on it and marks every section it can't check.

## Example requests

**Full analysis**
Hey Claude, I just added the "tiktok-ads-analyzer" skill. Review my TikTok Ads account for the last 30 days. My target cost per lead is $25.

**Top creatives**
Hey Claude, I just added the "tiktok-ads-analyzer" skill. Rank my TikTok ads by cost per result and tell me why the bottom ones lose: hook, hold, CTR or the page.

**Spark Ads versus ad-only posts**
Hey Claude, I just added the "tiktok-ads-analyzer" skill. Do my Spark Ads beat my ad-only posts on TikTok?

**Pangle and placements**
Hey Claude, I just added the "tiktok-ads-analyzer" skill. How much of my TikTok spend goes to Pangle, and is it worth it?

**Cost spike**
Hey Claude, I just added the "tiktok-ads-analyzer" skill. My TikTok cost per result jumped this week. Find out why.

**Monthly client report**
Hey Claude, I just added the "tiktok-ads-analyzer" skill. Write last month's TikTok Ads report for my client, with month-over-month changes and next steps.

## What you get

1. **A headline:** spend, impressions, clicks and CTR, then results, cost per result, purchase value and ROAS for each optimization goal.
2. **The creative funnel:** hook rate, 6-second hold, completion, CTR and results per click, and the first step that breaks.
3. **A top creatives leaderboard,** with a diagnosis for each weak ad.
4. **Comparisons:** Spark Ads against ad-only posts, Smart+ against manual campaigns, and one bid strategy against another, each compared only within the same goal.
5. **Who and where:** age, gender, region, targeting type, custom and lookalike audiences, placements including Pangle, and device.
6. **Checks:** cost-per-result spikes split into auction cost, creative and post-click causes, plus pixel setup, lead forms, TikTok Shop and catalog ads, and budget pacing with a month-end forecast.
7. **Up to 5 top actions, a testing agenda, and an honest list of what couldn't be graded,** with the reason for each.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can:
- pause an ad that loses on cost per result (never the last live ad in an ad group)
- change an ad group's daily budget

It doesn't change bids, targeting or placements. It recommends those changes.

## Tips

- Grades need volume: at least 1,000 impressions per ad and 10 results per comparison. Smaller numbers are shown as directional, not graded.
- If your account hasn't spent in the last 30 days, the skill finds the last month that did and labels the report as historical.
- Run it monthly, and again a week after any new creative batch.
