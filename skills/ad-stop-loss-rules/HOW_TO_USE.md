# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "ad-stop-loss-rules" skill. Set stop-loss rules for my new TikTok ads at a $40 target CPA, test them on the last 90 days, and tell me which live ads to turn off today.

## What you need

- **Your target cost per result:** the CPA, cost per lead or cost per install you can afford. Every rule is built from it.
- **Ad-level daily data for the last 90 days,** either:
  - an export from your ads manager (date, campaign, ad group, ad, spend, impressions, clicks, results), or
  - the InsightfulPipe MCP connected with TikTok Ads, LinkedIn Ads, Microsoft Ads, Snapchat Ads, Pinterest Ads or X Ads, so the skill pulls it itself.
- **Optional:** a daily cap per campaign, and how many results an ad needs before it counts as proven (default 3).

## Example requests

**Design and back-test**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ad-stop-loss-rules" skill. Our target is $55 per lead on LinkedIn and Microsoft Ads. Write stop-loss rules for new ads and show me which of last quarter's winners they would have killed.

**From an export, no connection**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ad-stop-loss-rules" skill. Here's a daily ad export from Snapchat for the last 90 days. Our target CPA is $30. How much should an ad spend before we kill it?

**Today's check**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ad-stop-loss-rules" skill. Run today's stop-loss check on my TikTok and Pinterest ads with the rules we agreed, and list what to stop.

**Save the rules on TikTok**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ad-stop-loss-rules" skill. Turn our stop-loss ladder into TikTok automated rules. Show me the payloads first.

## What you get

1. **The rules in plain money:** for example "no purchase by $120, off; one purchase by $189.60, off", plus a click-rate floor from your own account and an overspend cap per campaign.
2. **A back-test on your last 90 days:** three settings side by side, the past winners each would have stopped, the spend each would have saved, and the one to use.
3. **Today's check:** which live ads to stop now, which are on watch and how much more they can spend, and which have proven themselves.
4. **The set-up:** TikTok automated rule payloads, or a daily check for the other platforms, and the pause payloads, all marked "not run".

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can save the rules as TikTok automated rules and pause today's stops on TikTok, LinkedIn, Microsoft Ads, Snapchat, Pinterest or X (on X, at line item level). It never deletes an ad, never switches anything on and never touches budgets or bids.

## Tips

- Agree the rules before the next round of tests launches, not after the results are in.
- Rerun the back-test every quarter, or when your target changes.
- An ad that sits a little above target is never stopped by a stop-loss; treat it as a bid or creative question.
