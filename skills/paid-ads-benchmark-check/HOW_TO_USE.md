# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "paid-ads-benchmark-check" skill. Are my ad numbers good? Compare last month with my own history and with benchmarks for my industry.

## What you need

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude, with your ad accounts added** (Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn, TikTok, Microsoft Ads, Pinterest, Snapchat or X). The skill pulls the numbers itself.
- **Or your numbers pasted in:** platform, campaign type or objective, the month, spend, impressions, clicks, leads or sales, and revenue. Earlier months make the own-history comparison possible.
- **Your industry and main country,** so the skill can pick a fair published figure.
- **What counts as a result:** a lead, a sign-up or a sale.

## Example requests

**Every platform at once**
Hey Claude, I just added the "paid-ads-benchmark-check" skill. Check all my ad accounts against my own last six months and against published benchmarks. We're a home remodeling company in the US.

**Meta only**
Hey Claude, I just added the "paid-ads-benchmark-check" skill. What is a good CTR for Facebook ads, and is mine good? My cost per lead is creeping up too.

**Google Ads only**
Hey Claude, I just added the "paid-ads-benchmark-check" skill. Is my Google Ads cost per lead good for a law firm? Leave brand search out of it.

**LinkedIn**
Hey Claude, I just added the "paid-ads-benchmark-check" skill. Are our LinkedIn Ads CPC and CPM normal for single image ads in the US?

**From a paste, no connection**
Hey Claude, I just added the "paid-ads-benchmark-check" skill. Here are last month's numbers from Ads Manager: spend 3,200, impressions 140,000, link clicks 1,500, leads 61, lead objective, US, dental clinic. Is that good?

## What you get

1. **A one-line answer** to "are my numbers good?"
2. **A comparison table** for each part of your accounts (brand and non-brand search, prospecting and retargeting, lead and sales objectives): your number, your own 6-month median, and the published figure with its source, or the reason no fair figure exists.
3. **The two or three gaps worth the most money,** with what is driving each one and the skill that fixes it.
4. **A list of what wasn't compared,** and why.
5. **Every source** with its publisher, period and sample size.

## What it can change for you

Nothing. It reads and compares.

## Tips

- A number "in line" with a median is ordinary, not good: half of advertisers do better. Your own profit per lead or sale decides what you can afford.
- On Meta, say whether your CTR uses link clicks or all clicks. The skill pulls both when connected.
- Pinterest, Snapchat and X have no published benchmark the skill trusts yet, so they are compared with your own history only.
- Run it once a month, after the month closes, so late conversions have arrived.
