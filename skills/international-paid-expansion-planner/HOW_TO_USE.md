# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "international-paid-expansion-planner" skill. Which countries should our Google Ads and Meta ads open next, and how should we test them? We can spend $6,000 a month on new markets.

## What you need

- **Nothing connected is fine.** Paste or describe your country numbers (sessions, sales, orders), a Keyword Planner download for each country, and your target, and the skill works from that.
- **Better: the InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude** with GA4, Search Console, Google Ads, Meta Ads and your Shopify store. The skill then pulls demand by country, local search volume and CPC, Meta audience size and the pages that sell for you at home by itself. DataForSEO covers countries Keyword Planner doesn't.
- **Your target ROAS (stores) or target CPA,** or it uses your home non-brand search result.
- **Your limits:** the monthly budget for new markets, where you can ship or serve, the currencies your checkout shows, and the languages your team or translators can write.

## Example requests

**Rank the next countries**
Hey Claude, I just added the "international-paid-expansion-planner" skill. Rank the countries our Google Ads should open next. Our target ROAS is 3.5 and we ship to the EU, the UK, Canada and Australia.

**Plan the tests and budget**
Hey Claude, I just added the "international-paid-expansion-planner" skill. We've picked the UK and Germany. Plan the Google Ads and Meta test in each: budget per market, campaign setup, what to translate, and when to stop.

**International paid social**
Hey Claude, I just added the "international-paid-expansion-planner" skill. We only run Meta ads in the US. Which countries should our paid social go to next, and how big a test does each need?

**Lead gen or software**
Hey Claude, I just added the "international-paid-expansion-planner" skill. We sell project software with a $120 target CPA per trial. Here's our GA4 country report and a Keyword Planner export for five countries. Where should our PPC go next?

**Without a connection**
Hey Claude, I just added the "international-paid-expansion-planner" skill. Nothing is connected. Here are our sessions, sales and Keyword Planner numbers by country, pasted below. Rank them and plan the first wave.

## What you get

1. **A candidate table:** demand each country already sends, with sessions, sales, conversion rate against home, order value, browser language and checkout currency.
2. **Cost and size:** local search volume, a planned CPC scaled by how your account really buys at home, the clicks each market can supply, the planned ROAS or CPA, and the Meta audience.
3. **A status and rank for every country:** open, test with fixes, too small to read, not now, calibrate or excluded, each with the rule that set it.
4. **Launch waves and budget per market** that fit your monthly money, with start days and expected results.
5. **A plan per market:** ad language, Google and Meta campaign setup, the exact pages and ads to localise, and what must be fixed before day 1.
6. **Stop rules and a stay-on rule** with each market's own numbers, plus the metrics to read every week.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can put the plan into a new tab of your Google Sheet. It never creates campaigns, changes budgets, bids or targeting, or publishes ads.

## Tips

- Give it the countries you can't serve first; they are excluded whatever their numbers.
- For a market in another language, give it a native speaker's version of your best search terms, or let it use that country's own Search Console queries.
- Run it again when wave 1 reaches day 60, so wave 2 starts from real results.
