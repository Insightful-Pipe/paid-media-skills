# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "ad-policy-fixer" skill. My LinkedIn ad was rejected. Here's the notice and the ad: tell me why, fix it, and tell me what to send.

## What you need

- **The notice,** pasted or as a screenshot: the email, or the text your ads manager shows on the ad. Copy the platform's exact words.
- **The ad:** its text, headline, link, and the image or video (upload it or describe it).
- **What you're advertising** and where, plus the targeting if you have it.
- **Optional: the InsightfulPipe MCP connected** with your LinkedIn, TikTok, Microsoft, Pinterest, Snapchat, X or ChatGPT ad accounts. The skill then finds every rejected and stuck ad and every blocked account by itself, and reads the reason wherever the platform passes it (LinkedIn, Pinterest and Snapchat do). Reddit always works from your paste.

## Example requests

**One rejected ad**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ad-policy-fixer" skill. TikTok rejected my ad for "misleading claims". Here's the ad text and the video description. Rewrite it so it passes without losing the hook.

**Disapproved Bing ad**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ad-policy-fixer" skill. Microsoft Ads disapproved my responsive search ad for capitalization and a superlative. Here are the headlines. Fix them inside the character limits.

**Ad not approved, no reason given**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ad-policy-fixer" skill. My X ad was rejected and the email doesn't say why. Here's the post. What did it most likely hit?

**Restricted account**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ad-policy-fixer" skill. Our TikTok ad account was restricted for repeated violations. Here's the notice. What do we fix first, and can you write the appeal?

**Sweep everything**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ad-policy-fixer" skill. Check all my connected ad accounts outside Google and Meta for rejected, stuck or blocked ads, and fix what you can.

**Restricted category check**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ad-policy-fixer" skill. We're running job ads on LinkedIn and a credit card on TikTok. Is any of our targeting a problem?

## What you get

1. **Where each account stands,** platform by platform.
2. **One list of every blocked ad,** in the order to fix them: blocked accounts first, then ads that left a campaign with nothing running. Each reason is marked as from the platform, from your notice, or likely.
3. **A card per rejected ad:** the exact words that tripped review, the policy page, the rewrite (old and new, with character counts), what to change in the image, and the route: edit, replace, request a review, wait or stop.
4. **A restricted category check** for housing, employment, credit, health, alcohol, gambling and political ads, listing every age, gender, ZIP or income narrowing it finds.
5. **Drafts:** review requests, a support message for a stuck ad, and the appeal for a blocked account.
6. **The exact changes it can make for you,** marked "not run".

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, and a Read & Write connection, it can:
- edit a rejected TikTok ad's text, or appeal a TikTok Smart+ ad
- edit a Snapchat headline or a ChatGPT ad's creative
- add a replacement LinkedIn creative, Microsoft ad (paused), Pinterest Pin and ad (paused), or X post

It never changes targeting, never switches on a paused ad or campaign, and sends no appeal for you outside TikTok Smart+. A replacement LinkedIn creative or X post goes to review in its running campaign and serves once approved, and it tells you that before you say yes.

## Tips

- Paste the platform's exact wording. A "likely" reason is an educated guess; the notice is the real one.
- Don't edit an ad that's stuck in review. On most platforms an edit restarts the review.
- Fix the pattern before resubmitting. Repeated rejections can get the whole account restricted.
- No one can promise a platform will approve an ad or lift a restriction. The skill gives you the strongest honest case.
- Facebook and Instagram ads are in `meta-ad-rejection-fixer`; Google Ads is in `google-ads-disapproval-suspension-fixer`.
