# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "snapchat-ads-manager" skill. Review my Snapchat Ads account for the last 30 days and tell me what to fix first.

## What you need

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude, with your Snapchat Ads account added.** The skill pulls the data itself; you don't export anything.
- **Your target cost per result** (or target ROAS for catalog campaigns), if you have one. Without it, each campaign is judged against its own average.
- **A brief,** only if you want a new campaign drafted: goal, audience, daily budget, landing page and start date.

## Example requests

**Full review**
Hey Claude, I just added the "snapchat-ads-manager" skill. Review my Snapchat Ads account for the last 30 days and tell me what to fix first.

**Monthly client report**
Hey Claude, I just added the "snapchat-ads-manager" skill. Write last month's Snapchat Ads report for my client, with spend, swipe-ups and cost per result against the month before.

**Pixel check**
Hey Claude, I just added the "snapchat-ads-manager" skill. Check whether my Snap Pixel is attached and actually recording conversions.

**New headlines**
Hey Claude, I just added the "snapchat-ads-manager" skill. Write five new Snapchat headlines and a 6-second script from my best ad.

**Campaign from a brief**
Hey Claude, I just added the "snapchat-ads-manager" skill. Draft a Snapchat sign-up campaign for our lookalike audience at $40 a day that sends people to our sign-up page. Show me the payloads before you create anything.

**Pause the losers**
Hey Claude, I just added the "snapchat-ads-manager" skill. Find the Snapchat ads that are wasting money and list them. I'll tell you which ones to pause.

## What you get

1. **A KPI table** for the period next to the period before: spend, impressions, reach, frequency, swipes, swipe-up rate, results and cost per result.
2. **A scorecard for eight areas:** delivery and spend, Snap Pixel and Conversions API, swipe-ups and attention, video completion, placements, audiences, cost per result trend, and Dynamic Product Ads. The Conversions API can't be read through the connection, so it comes as a checklist for Snapchat Events Manager.
3. **A grade out of 100** with the math shown, or "not graded" when most areas can't be judged, from too little data or data the connection can't reach.
4. **Up to five fixes,** ranked by the spend they affect, each with the numbers behind it.
5. **A table of every ad** with its swipe-up rate, screen time and cost per result.
6. **New headlines (34 characters max) and short scripts,** built from the ad that works best.
7. **A list of what couldn't be checked,** and why.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact payload, it can:
- create a campaign, ad squad, creative and ad from your brief, always paused, so you switch them on and keep them running
- pause an ad that meets the pause rule
- change a creative's headline (the ads using it go back into review)

It never switches anything on, and never edits budgets, bids or targeting on live ad squads.

## Tips

- Snapchat finalises stats about 48 hours after each day, so the last two days of a report can still move.
- Age, gender and location splits aren't available through the connection yet; the skill shows your targeting instead.
- If nothing spent in the period, the skill says so and offers to review the last campaign that did.
