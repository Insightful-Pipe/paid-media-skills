# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "newsletter-podcast-ads-planner" skill. Here are three newsletter quotes and two podcast media kits; tell me which to book at my $120 target cost per order.

## What you need

- **Your target cost per order or per lead.** Every price, counter-offer and rebook rule is set from it.
- **The quotes and media kits,** pasted or attached: price, sends or episodes, slot, audience breakdown, and any proof the publisher sent (sponsor click screenshots, a downloads dashboard).
- **Your budget and dates.**
- **A short list of facts you can stand behind** (shipping, returns, warranty, offer). The copy never goes beyond them.
- **Optional, through the InsightfulPipe MCP:** GA4, Shopify or WooCommerce, CallRail, Search Console, Google Ads, Meta Ads, YouTube and Google Sheets. With them, the skill reads your past placements' results and your winning ad lines itself. Without them, paste the same numbers.

## Example requests

**Compare quotes**
Hey Claude, I just added the "newsletter-podcast-ads-planner" skill. Compare these five sponsorship quotes for November. My target cost per order is $120 and the budget is $6,500.

**Is this rate fair?**
Hey Claude, I just added the "newsletter-podcast-ads-planner" skill. A podcast quoted a $25 CPM on 18,000 downloads per episode for a host-read mid-roll. Is that a fair podcast advertising cost for us, and what should I counter with?

**Did it work?**
Hey Claude, I just added the "newsletter-podcast-ads-planner" skill. Our three September sponsorships have finished. Read the results from GA4, Shopify and CallRail and tell me which to book again.

**Write the copy**
Hey Claude, I just added the "newsletter-podcast-ads-planner" skill. Write the 100-word newsletter blurb and the host-read talking points for Hybrid Hours, using the ad lines that work best in our Google Ads and Meta accounts.

## What you get

1. **A readout of past placements:** orders by code, tagged link and vanity URL, calls, a check on the publisher's clicks, branded search lift with a noise floor, and a rebook, retest or stop verdict.
2. **A quote comparison:** cost per thousand delivered and per thousand people who match your buyer, which claims are proven, expected orders from your own past results, a break-even price, and a book, counter or skip decision with the counter price.
3. **A budget plan** that fills your budget in order of expected cost per order.
4. **The stop-or-rebook rule for each buy,** written before you pay.
5. **A tracking table:** tagged links, a new code per buy, vanity URLs and call tracking numbers for podcasts.
6. **The copy:** newsletter blurbs led by your best ad lines (two variants for a placement you book, one for a counter-offer), inside each publication's word limit, and host-read talking points with a spoken disclosure, the code and vanity URL, and your call tracking number if you take calls.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, and a Read & Write connection, it can:
- create the new placement codes in WooCommerce
- add a CallRail tracking number for a podcast (a new number can add to your CallRail bill)
- write the plan, rules and tracking into your placement log in Google Sheets

It never books, pays for or sends anything to a publisher. Shopify codes are made in Shopify admin.

## Tips

- Ask every publisher for proof before you talk price: sponsor clicks from their last few sends, or 30-day downloads per episode from their host's dashboard.
- Give each buy its own code and tagged link, even when you rebook the same newsletter.
- Run the readout when the read window closes: 7 days after a newsletter send, 30 days after a podcast episode.
- Podcast listeners often search for you instead of clicking. Keep your brand campaign running steadily during a test so the lift can be seen.
