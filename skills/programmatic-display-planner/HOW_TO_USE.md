# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "programmatic-display-planner" skill. Plan a programmatic display campaign for our holiday sale: $36,000 over six weeks, US only, and here are the three DSP quotes we got.

## What you need

- **Your budget, flight dates and goal** (orders or leads, site visits, or awareness).
- **The DSP quotes,** pasted: monthly minimum, platform fee, add-on fees, deal types, and the CPMs their team forecasts. The skill never guesses a DSP's prices.
- **Your creative on hand:** display sizes and video lengths.
- **For a review,** the DSP's delivery export by line: spend, impressions, measured and viewable impressions, invalid traffic, clicks, conversions, reach, and video starts and completes.
- **Optional, through the InsightfulPipe MCP:** GA4, Google Ads and Meta Ads. With them, the skill sizes your retargeting pool, sets cost ceilings from your own ad costs, and checks the DSP's clicks and conversions against GA4. Without them, paste the same numbers.

## Example requests

**Plan a flight**
Hey Claude, I just added the "programmatic-display-planner" skill. We sell e-bikes online and want to try programmatic advertising for the first time. Budget is $36,000 from November 3 to December 14. Build the plan and tell me how to buy programmatic ads without wasting it.

**Pick a DSP**
Hey Claude, I just added the "programmatic-display-planner" skill. Here are quotes from three DSPs with their minimums and fees. Which one fits a $25,000 a month budget, and what will a thousand impressions really cost on each?

**Review a running campaign**
Hey Claude, I just added the "programmatic-display-planner" skill. Here is our DSP export for September. Check viewability, invalid traffic and frequency, compare the clicks with GA4, and tell me which lines to keep, fix or cut.

**Review, then plan the next flight**
Hey Claude, I just added the "programmatic-display-planner" skill. Review our September pilot from this export, then plan the holiday flight on what it showed.

## What you get

1. **Cost ceilings from your own numbers:** a CPA ceiling, a viewable CPM ceiling and a cost per engaged session ceiling, each with its source.
2. **A DSP pick** on all-in cost per thousand impressions, or a plain "not yet" when the budget doesn't fit programmatic.
3. **Retargeting sized from GA4,** capped at what your visitors can absorb at the frequency cap, with recent buyers and customers excluded.
4. **The line plan:** deal type, inventory, audience, budget, all-in CPM, expected impressions, minimum reach, tagged link, KPI, and the highest CPM a line can pay and still hit its ceiling (for video, and for lines your last flight already tested).
5. **Written standards and supply rules:** MRC viewability, invalid traffic filtering, frequency caps, ads.txt and sellers.json checks, and made-for-advertising exclusions.
6. **A review of each line:** viewability, invalid traffic, frequency, GA4 sessions per click, confirmed conversions and a keep, retune, fix supply, cut or too early verdict.

## What it can change for you

Nothing. There is no DSP connector, so you or your DSP team make every change. The skill gives you the exact settings for each line.

## Tips

- Ask each DSP for its pixel match share; it decides how much retargeting you can buy.
- Give every line its own tagged link, so GA4 can check the DSP's clicks and conversions.
- Judge on click-through conversions that GA4 also sees. Treat view-through conversions as a question for a holdout test.
- Keep 10% back until the week-3 review, then give it to the lines that earned it.
