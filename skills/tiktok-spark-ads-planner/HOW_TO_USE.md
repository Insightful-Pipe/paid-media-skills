# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "tiktok-spark-ads-planner" skill. Which of our TikToks should we run as Spark Ads next, and how should we set them up?

## What you need

- **Your goal, budget and dates:** for example online sales, $1,400 over 14 days from November 2.
- **Your posts.** With TikTok Pages and TikTok Ads connected through the InsightfulPipe MCP, the skill reads your posts, the ads already running and your own cost per result itself. Without a connection, paste your post list from TikTok Studio (views, likes, comments, shares, date).
- **A TikTok Studio export,** if you can download one. It adds the share of viewers who watched each video to the end, which TikTok Pages doesn't return.
- **Creator posts,** if any: the link, the post's numbers, the creator's usual numbers, and each Spark authorization code with the length they picked and the day they made it.

## Example requests

**Pick the posts**
Hey Claude, I just added the "tiktok-spark-ads-planner" skill. Rank our TikToks from the last 90 days and tell me which ones to run as Spark Ads for website sales.

**Creator codes**
Hey Claude, I just added the "tiktok-spark-ads-planner" skill. Three creators sent us Spark codes. Log them, tell me which ones will run out before our campaign ends, and write the message asking for a new code.

**Plan and draft**
Hey Claude, I just added the "tiktok-spark-ads-planner" skill. We have $100 a day for two weeks. Plan the Spark ad group and draft the ads, but show me everything before you create anything.

**No connection**
Hey Claude, I just added the "tiktok-spark-ads-planner" skill. Here's my TikTok Studio export. Which posts are worth paying to spark for more followers?

## What you get

1. **The bar, stated first:** the view floor, your account's usual share, comment, like and watched-full rates, and the rule a post must clear (top quarter of its pool and a score of at least 1.20).
2. **Every post ranked** with its indexes against your usual, a score, Spark, Backup or Skip, and any hold: a popular song that may be blocked in ads, a post already running, a dated offer, a missing or expiring code.
3. **An authorization code log:** each creator's code, when it runs out, and whether it covers the whole run, plus a ready-to-send message for any code you still need.
4. **The plan:** ad group goal, audience, TikTok-only placement, daily budget, run dates, expected purchases (as an estimate), and whether the budget is enough for TikTok's learning phase.
5. **The exact ad group and ad payloads,** marked not run.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact payloads, it can create the Spark campaign (only if you have no fitting one), the ad group and the ads. TikTok Ads creates them disabled; you review and switch them on in TikTok Ads Manager. A creator's code has to be redeemed in TikTok Ads Manager first, because the connector has no call for it.

## Tips

- Run it every two weeks: organic posts that take off are the best Spark candidates, and they go stale.
- Ask creators for codes that cover the whole run plus a few days. A code that runs out stops the ad.
- After a week of spend, ask tiktok-ads-analyzer (if installed) how the Spark ads did against your other ads.
