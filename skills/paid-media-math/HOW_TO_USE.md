# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "paid-media-math" skill. Work out my break-even ROAS and the most I can pay for a customer, then tell me how much I need to spend to hit my revenue goal next month.

## What you need

- **Nothing connected, if you prefer.** Type your average order value, product cost, shipping and packing cost, card fees and refund rate, and the skill does the rest. For lead generation, give your close rate and what a sale leaves after delivery costs.
- **Optional: the InsightfulPipe MCP** with your store (Shopify, WooCommerce or Magento), your ad accounts (Google Ads, Meta, Microsoft Ads, LinkedIn Ads, TikTok Ads), GA4 or CallRail. The skill then pulls order value, refunds, repeat orders, spend, clicks, conversions and your live bid targets itself, and shows you what it pulled so you can correct it.
- **Your goal,** if you want a budget: revenue, orders or leads, and the period.

## Example requests

**Break-even ROAS**
Hey Claude, I just added the "paid-media-math" skill. My average order is $62, products cost $21, shipping and packing is $9, card fees are 3% plus 30 cents and 4% of revenue is refunded. What's my break-even ROAS and ACOS?

**Target CPA**
Hey Claude, I just added the "paid-media-math" skill. What's the most we can pay to get a new customer if we want our money back within 6 months?

**Budget for a goal**
Hey Claude, I just added the "paid-media-math" skill. We want $80,000 in revenue from ads next month. How much do we need to spend on Google and Meta at today's rates?

**Is this target realistic?**
Hey Claude, I just added the "paid-media-math" skill. My boss wants a 4.0 ROAS on Meta. Does that make money, and can we actually get there?

**Check live targets**
Hey Claude, I just added the "paid-media-math" skill. Check every target ROAS, target CPA and cost cap in my ad accounts and tell me which ones lose money if they're hit.

**Test cost**
Hey Claude, I just added the "paid-media-math" skill. We want to test a new landing page on Google Ads. How much will it cost and how long will it take before we know if it's 10% better?

## What you get

1. **An inputs sheet:** every number used, with where it came from (typed, pulled or a default).
2. **The targets:** break-even ROAS on net revenue and on the basis your ad platforms report, break-even ACOS, the ROAS for your profit goal, and the first-order, payback and profit-goal CPA, each with its formula.
3. **Your accounts against them:** CPA and ROAS per channel with a verdict, plus MER, profit after ads, blended CAC and how many orders a new customer takes to pay back.
4. **A goal plan:** orders, conversions, clicks and budget per channel to reach your goal, with a range when volumes are small.
5. **Target checks:** whether a target makes money if it's hit, whether it can be reached at today's rates, and which number has to move how far.
6. **Live target flags:** every bid target in your accounts that sits on the losing side of break-even, with the value that pays back.
7. **Test cost:** clicks, spend and days a test needs, and the smallest lift your budget can detect in 28 days.

## What it can change for you

Nothing. It reads and calculates. When a bid target is flagged, it tells you the value that pays back and leaves the change to you.

## Tips

- Give the product cost per order honestly, landed. Everything rests on it, and the skill won't guess it.
- Say whether your ad platforms receive the order total with tax and shipping or net revenue. If you don't know, connect the store and the skill works it out.
- Run it again when prices, costs or shipping change, and before you set a new target ROAS or cost cap.
