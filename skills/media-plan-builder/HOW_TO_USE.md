# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "media-plan-builder" skill. We launch in the US on 4 January with $45,000 for January to March. Build the paid media plan: which platforms, budget by month, what each line should deliver, and a tracker sheet.

## What you need

- **A brief.** What you sell and for how much, your gross margin, the result you want the ads to drive, the budget, the dates and the countries. The skill asks for anything missing and uses a stated default when you don't know.
- **Optional: the InsightfulPipe MCP** with Google Ads, Meta Ads, LinkedIn Ads or TikTok Ads added. The skill then reads Keyword Planner volumes and bids, Meta audience sizes and minimum budgets, LinkedIn suggested bids and TikTok bid recommendations itself. Nothing connected is fine: it works from your numbers and labelled assumptions.
- **Optional: a Google Sheet** if you want the plan written there as a tracker.

## Example requests

**A launch plan**
Hey Claude, I just added the "media-plan-builder" skill. We launch our app for restaurants in the US on 4 January with $45,000 for the first quarter. Build the media plan.

**Where should a new business start?**
Hey Claude, I just added the "media-plan-builder" skill. We're a new online store with $3,000 a month for ads. Which ad platforms should we start with, and how should we split the first month?

**A seasonal flight**
Hey Claude, I just added the "media-plan-builder" skill. Plan a six-week burst for our spring sale across Google and Meta, with $20,000 and most of it in the first two weeks.

**Plan, then the sheet**
Hey Claude, I just added the "media-plan-builder" skill. Build the plan, then put it in my Google Sheet "Marketing 2027" as a tracker. Show me what you'll write first.

## What you get

1. **Success numbers:** the most you can pay per result, and the target to plan on, from your own margin and customer value.
2. **Platforms:** run, test or not now, each with the reason in numbers, and when to look again at the ones left out.
3. **Line floors:** a check that every line can buy enough results each week for the platform to learn, and the fix when it can't.
4. **Budget by platform and month,** with the flighting (always-on, burst or pulsed) and a reserve with a rule for releasing it.
5. **A forecast per line and month:** impressions, reach, frequency, clicks, results and cost per result, with the assumption behind each number.
6. **The creative each line needs,** with due dates.
7. **Launch gates** for day 3 and weeks 2, 4 and 6, written before any money is spent.
8. **A tracker sheet** with planned figures, columns for actuals and formulas that grade each line.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, it can add new tabs to your Google Sheet and fill them with the plan and the assumptions. It never overwrites a tab, and it creates no campaigns or budgets in your ad accounts.

## Tips

- Give your real conversion rates (from GA4 or past campaigns) where you have them; every one replaces an assumption.
- If you already have months of spend on these platforms, ask for the budget split from your own results instead (the budget allocator skill, if installed), and use this skill for the new lines.
- Rerun the forecast at week 2 with real CPMs, click rates and costs; the plan tells you which numbers to swap.
