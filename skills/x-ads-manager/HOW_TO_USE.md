# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "x-ads-manager" skill. Review my X Ads account for the last 30 days and tell me what to fix first.

## What you need

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude, with your X Ads account added.** The skill pulls the data itself; you don't need to export anything.
- **The result that counts for you** (link clicks, conversions, engagements, follows, video views), if it isn't the goal already set on your line items.
- **For a new campaign:** the goal, the audience, the daily budget, the landing page and the facts the posts may use.

## Example requests

**Account review**
Hey Claude, I just added the "x-ads-manager" skill. Review my X Ads account for the last 30 days. Which promoted posts have the weakest engagement rate, and is my cost per engagement going up?

**Targeting**
Hey Claude, I just added the "x-ads-manager" skill. Compare my keyword, follower lookalike and conversation line items. Which audience gets the cheapest link clicks?

**Video and pixel**
Hey Claude, I just added the "x-ads-manager" skill. How far do people watch my X video ads, and is the X Pixel recording conversions?

**Monthly client report**
Hey Claude, I just added the "x-ads-manager" skill. Write this month's X Ads report for my client, compared with the month before.

**New campaign and posts**
Hey Claude, I just added the "x-ads-manager" skill. Draft a website-clicks campaign for US fitness studios and personal trainers at $20 a day, with three post variations. Show me the payloads first and don't run anything yet.

**Pause what isn't working**
Hey Claude, I just added the "x-ads-manager" skill. Find the line items whose promoted posts all have low engagement and offer to pause them.

## What you get

1. **A scorecard for six areas:** readiness and delivery, promoted post engagement, targeting (keywords, lookalikes, conversations), video completion, X Pixel and conversions, and the cost per engagement trend. Each says pass, warn or fail with the numbers behind it, or "not graded" and why.
2. **A grade out of 100** when at least four areas have enough data, with the math shown.
3. **The top fixes,** each with its evidence and who does it.
4. **A client report** on request: three plain sentences, a KPI table against the previous 30 days and the top posts.
5. **A drafted campaign:** the campaign, one line item per audience, the targeting and the promoted posts, as exact payloads built from your account.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on each exact payload, it can create a campaign, line items, targeting and promoted-only posts, all paused, and pause a line item or campaign. It never switches anything on, never changes budgets or bids, and never deletes. You switch the campaign on in X Ads Manager after checking it.

## Tips

- X can't split results by single keyword or conversation topic, so keep one audience type per line item. The skill drafts campaigns that way, so next month's review can compare them.
- The connection can't read the text of your promoted posts. Paste your best posts' text when you ask for new variations.
- Pixel tags are managed in X Events Manager; the skill reads what your line items and results show.
