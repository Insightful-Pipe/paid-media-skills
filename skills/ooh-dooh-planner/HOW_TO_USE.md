# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "ooh-dooh-planner" skill. Plan a 4-week billboard and digital out-of-home push for our spring sale with a $60,000 budget, using our GA4 sales by city and the quotes I'm pasting.

## What you need

- **Your demand by city and week,** for at least 13 weeks. Either connect GA4 (and Search Console, and Google Business Profile if you have stores) through the InsightfulPipe MCP, or paste orders or leads by city or ZIP and week.
- **The media owners' quotes:** market, format, placement, weekly impressions for adults 18+, whether they are audited, 4-week media cost, production and install, and spot and loop length for screens. Add the 18+ population each owner used.
- **Your budget (all in), flight dates and profit per purchase or lead.** Profit per sale sets the break-even lift; without it you get lift in units only.
- **Optional:** your target weekly GRPs (default 50), a DSP forecast for programmatic DOOH, and your creative drafts with hex colours and letter heights.

## Example requests

**Full plan**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ooh-dooh-planner" skill. We have $60,000 for billboards and screens around Presidents' Day. Pick the cities from our GA4 sales, price these quotes, add programmatic DOOH if it makes sense, and tell me how we'll measure it.

**Which cities to buy**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ooh-dooh-planner" skill. Here are our orders by ZIP for the last 4 months. Which markets are worth billboards, and which ones could we actually measure?

**Compare quotes**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ooh-dooh-planner" skill. Three media owners sent quotes for Atlanta. Rank the boards by all-in CPM and tell me the cheapest package that reaches 40 weekly GRPs.

**Check the creative**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ooh-dooh-planner" skill. Check these three billboard and transit drafts for word count, contrast and letter height, and fix the ones that fail.

**Measurement plan only**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ooh-dooh-planner" skill. We're buying boards in Charlotte and Raleigh next month. Pick control markets from our GA4 data and tell me the smallest lift we could see and the lift we need to break even.

## What you get

1. **A markets table** built from your own purchases or leads, with the ones too small to read set aside.
2. **A package per quoted market:** the audited units that reach your GRP target at the lowest all-in CPM, and what was left out.
3. **Funding decisions** in order of purchases per $1,000, each with a control market or the reason it wasn't bought.
4. **Programmatic DOOH lines** geofenced to the funded markets, or a reserve and why.
5. **A creative check** for every draft, with a fixed version where one fails.
6. **The lift plan:** control, fit, placebo, smallest readable lift and break-even lift per market and pooled, plus short paths and promo codes.
7. **A budget summary and a dated calendar** from contracts to the read date.

## What it can change for you

Nothing. It reads your data and writes the plan. You book the boards and the programmatic line.

## Tips

- Ask media owners for audited impressions; unaudited counts are listed but not priced.
- Keep city-level budgets in other channels steady in test and control markets during the freeze, or the read can't separate the boards from the change.
- After the flight, read the result against this plan with the TV, Offline Media and Geo Lift Analyst skill.
