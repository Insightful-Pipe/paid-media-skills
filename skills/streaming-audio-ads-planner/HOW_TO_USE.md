# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "streaming-audio-ads-planner" skill. Plan a 4-week Spotify ads test in Colorado, Oregon and Minnesota with $16,000, and tell me how we'll know it worked.

## What you need

- **Your target cost per new customer** (a first-time buyer, or a new lead). The decision rule is set from it.
- **Budget, flight dates and the regions** where the audio will run, plus a few similar regions where it won't.
- **The quotes:** for Spotify, the CPM and minimum budget Ads Manager shows for each ad set's targeting and its audience estimate; for other sellers or a DSP, their CPM, data fee, platform fee, minimum spend, and whether they report reach and frequency.
- **After the flight:** the delivery export per ad set or line, and the codes and vanity URL you used.
- **Optional, through the InsightfulPipe MCP:** GA4, Google Ads, Search Console, and Shopify or WooCommerce. With them, the skill pulls the regional baseline, the brand impressions and the code orders itself. Without them, paste the same numbers.

## Example requests

**How to advertise on Spotify**
Hey Claude, I just added the "streaming-audio-ads-planner" skill. We sell coffee subscriptions online. How should we advertise on Spotify with $16,000 over four weeks, and how will we know it worked?

**What Spotify ads cost us**
Hey Claude, I just added the "streaming-audio-ads-planner" skill. Here are the CPMs Ads Manager shows for three ad sets, a streaming radio seller's rate card and a DSP quote for programmatic audio advertising. Which ones are worth buying after fees?

**Write the spot**
Hey Claude, I just added the "streaming-audio-ads-planner" skill. Lay out a 30-second Spotify spot and the companion banner for our first-order offer, with a code per ad set.

**Did it work?**
Hey Claude, I just added the "streaming-audio-ads-planner" skill. The flight ended a week ago. Here is the Ads Manager export; read GA4, Google Ads and Shopify and tell me whether to scale, retest or stop.

## What you get

1. **Every quote priced after fees** as an effective CPM, and checked for state targeting, reach and frequency reporting, and a minimum spend that fits.
2. **A budget split** from the cheapest qualified line up, with planned impressions, reach and frequency per line, and no line asked to reach more than 75% of its audience.
3. **The spot and companion:** a 30-second script within Spotify's word guidance with the vanity URL and the line's code each said twice, a 15-second cut, and the companion image, tagline and advertiser name checked against Spotify's music and podcast specs.
4. **The baseline before launch:** control regions picked from the data, leaving out any that share a metro area with a test region, a noise floor for new users, first-time buyers and brand impressions, the break-even lift, whether the test can be read, and the scale, retest or stop rule.
5. **The readout:** delivery against plan, code orders by line with leakage outside the test regions, vanity URL visits, lift against control, cost per incremental first-time buyer, and the verdict.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact change, and a Read & Write connection, it can create one WooCommerce coupon per line before launch. Shopify codes are made in Shopify admin. Buying the media, uploading the audio and setting frequency caps stay in Ads Manager or the seller's tool.

## Tips

- Keep your Google Ads brand campaign steady (same bids and budget) through the baseline and the flight, so its impressions measure the audio.
- Give each ad set its own code; the script stays the same and only the spoken code changes.
- Make the vanity path a real page on your site, not a redirect, so GA4 sees it.
- Don't add or pause other channels in only the test regions during the flight.
