# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "ctv-streaming-ads-planner" skill. Plan a 4-week connected TV test in Tennessee and Kentucky with $48,000, tell me where to buy, and how we'll know it worked.

## What you need

- **Your target cost per new customer** and what counts as one (a first order, a sign-up or a lead). The decision rule is built from it.
- **Budget, flight dates and the regions** where the ads will run, plus a few similar regions where they won't.
- **The quotes:** for each route (YouTube on TV screens, a streaming service's self-serve tool, a DSP for programmatic CTV), the CPM, any data and platform fees, the minimum spend, the audience estimate in your test regions, and whether it can target only those regions, cap frequency, and report reach, frequency and impressions by region.
- **After the flight:** each seller's delivery export.
- **Optional, through the InsightfulPipe MCP:** Google Ads, GA4 and Search Console. With them, the skill prices YouTube from your own TV-screen CPM, pulls the regional baseline and the branded-search series, and counts QR sessions itself. Without them, paste the same numbers.

## Example requests

**CTV advertising strategy**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ctv-streaming-ads-planner" skill. We sell fresh dog food on subscription. Is CTV advertising worth testing for us with $48,000, and what would the plan look like?

**How to buy connected TV ads**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ctv-streaming-ads-planner" skill. Here are quotes from a streaming app's self-serve tool, a DSP and a FAST channel network. Compare them with YouTube on TV screens after fees and tell me which to fund.

**YouTube on TV screens today**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ctv-streaming-ads-planner" skill. Read my Google Ads video campaigns and tell me how much already runs on TV screens, at what CPM and completion rate.

**How to measure CTV ads**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ctv-streaming-ads-planner" skill. Set up the measurement before we launch: control states, baseline, noise floors and the rule for scaling or stopping.

**Did it work?**
Hey Claude, I just added the "ctv-streaming-ads-planner" skill. The read window closed. Here are the delivery exports; read GA4 and Google Ads and tell me whether to scale, retest or stop.

## What you get

1. **Every route priced as an effective CPM after fees,** and checked for region targeting, a frequency cap, and reach, frequency and region reporting.
2. **A budget split** from the cheapest measurable route up, with planned impressions, household reach and frequency per route, and the money no route can use reported, not forced in.
3. **Combined household reach** across routes, with the homes two sellers both reach and how many ads they can see.
4. **Creative specs:** 30- and 15-second cuts, a QR code per route with its own tags, on-screen size and timing, and a short URL page.
5. **The measurement plan:** control regions screened for shared DMAs and size and picked by the data, a noise floor for new customers, Direct new users and branded searches, the break-even lift, and the scale, retest or stop rule.
6. **The readout:** delivery against plan, QR sessions per route, lift against control, cost per incremental new customer and the verdict.

## What it can change for you

Nothing. It reads only. You buy the media and build the campaigns in Google Ads or each seller's tool.

## Tips

- Keep your Google Ads brand campaign steady (same bids and budget) from the baseline to the end of the read window, so its impressions measure branded searches.
- Ask each seller whether its audience estimate counts households or people, and for its spec sheet.
- Don't change prices or other channels in only the test regions during the flight.
