# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "tiktok-ads-campaign-builder" skill. Build a TikTok campaign that sends US freelancers to my site to sign up, $50 a day, and show me everything before you create it.

## What you need

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude, with your TikTok Ads account added.** The skill reads the account itself; you don't need to export anything.
- **A short brief:** what you sell, who buys it, the landing page, the goal, the daily budget and the countries.
- **Facts the ads may use,** such as offers, numbers or reviews. Without them, captions only say what your brief and your past ads already say.
- **A target cost per result,** if you have one. Without it, the skill uses your own 30-day average.

## Example requests

**Build a campaign from a brief**
Hey Claude, I just added the "tiktok-ads-campaign-builder" skill. Create a TikTok traffic campaign for our new landing page, US only, $60 a day. Draft it and wait for my yes.

**Write captions and CTAs**
Hey Claude, I just added the "tiktok-ads-campaign-builder" skill. Write five TikTok ad captions and a CTA for our sign-up offer, plus three video hooks.

**Pause tired creatives**
Hey Claude, I just added the "tiktok-ads-campaign-builder" skill. Which of my TikTok ads are fatigued? Compare this week with last week and tell me what to pause.

**Scale what works**
Hey Claude, I just added the "tiktok-ads-campaign-builder" skill. Find the TikTok ad groups that beat a $25 cost per lead and are held back by budget, and propose new budgets.

## What you get

1. **An account read:** what has really spent, what is delivering, and the pieces it can reuse (your TikTok identity, videos, pixel and tracking link).
2. **A campaign plan:** objective and why, structure, a budget check against TikTok's minimums, confirmed locations, and broad or interest targeting with the reason.
3. **Five captions** inside TikTok's 12 to 100 character limit, each on a different angle, plus a CTA and three video hooks.
4. **The exact payloads** for the campaign, ad group and ads, marked not run until you say yes.
5. **A fatigue table** for live ads (this week against last week) and **a scaling table** for live ad groups, with proposed pauses and budget raises.

## What it can change for you

With your yes on the exact payload, it can:
- create the campaign, ad groups and ads (the InsightfulPipe MCP always creates them switched off, for safety)
- pause fatigued ads
- raise a winning ad group's daily budget by one step (20% by default)

It never switches anything on, deletes anything, or changes bids or targeting on a live ad group.

## Tips

- Upload two or three new videos before launch if the account has only one. On TikTok the video does most of the work.
- Run the fatigue check weekly once ads are live, and the scaling check 48 hours after each budget raise.
- If you track sign-ups with the TikTok pixel, tell it the event name so the campaign can optimize for sign-ups instead of clicks.
