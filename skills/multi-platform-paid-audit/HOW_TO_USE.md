# How to Use This Skill

Hey Claude, I just added the "multi-platform-paid-audit" skill. Audit all my ad platforms for the last 30 days and tell me where the money is going wrong.

## What you need

- **The InsightfulPipe MCP connected to Claude, with your ad accounts added:** any of Google Ads, Meta, Microsoft Ads, LinkedIn Ads, TikTok Ads, Pinterest Ads, Snapchat Ads, X Ads and OpenAI Ads. GA4 for the same site makes the audit much stronger, because it gives one count every platform is checked against.
- **No connection?** Upload each platform's campaign or ad group export, the ad list with created dates, the targeting and the conversion settings, plus a GA4 source/medium report. The skill runs the same checks on the files.
- **The result that matters** (purchase, lead, booked call) and the countries you sell to, if they aren't obvious.

## Example requests

**Full paid media audit**
Hey Claude, I just added the "multi-platform-paid-audit" skill. Run a paid media audit across Google, Meta, Microsoft and TikTok for the last 30 days. We sell to the US only.

**Which platform is wasting money**
Hey Claude, I just added the "multi-platform-paid-audit" skill. Every platform says it's doing great but sales are flat. Compare them on our real purchase count and show me which one is over-claiming.

**Paid social audit**
Hey Claude, I just added the "multi-platform-paid-audit" skill. Audit just our paid social: Meta, TikTok, Pinterest and Snapchat. Check creative age, retargeting overlap and whether prospecting excludes our customers.

**From exports**
Hey Claude, I just added the "multi-platform-paid-audit" skill. I've attached the Google Ads and Meta exports and a GA4 report. Run the cross-platform audit on these files.

## What you get

1. **A score out of 100 for each platform and one overall score,** weighted by spend, with the coverage stated.
2. **A side-by-side table of how each platform counts a conversion:** what it optimises for, its click and view windows, and how far its count sits from your site's count.
3. **Budget fit:** each platform's share of spend against its share of real results, and where the next dollar should go.
4. **Audiences bought twice:** how many retargeting ads each site visitor sees across all platforms, and which prospecting ad groups still reach your customers.
5. **Settings compared:** countries, location options and ages that differ between platforms.
6. **Fixes ranked by the money at stake,** including the top fixes from any one-platform audit skill you have installed.
7. **A list of what couldn't be checked,** and why.

## What it can change for you

It is an audit and changes nothing on its own. With your yes on the exact ad or ad set, it can pause one on Meta. Turning it back on is done in Meta Ads Manager.

## Tips

- Install the one-platform audits (for example google-ads-account-audit or facebook-ads-account-auditor). This skill runs them and adds their findings instead of repeating them.
- Tell it which platforms are there for reach or prospecting. It will suggest a holdout test there instead of a cut.
- Run it monthly, and again after the fixes to see the scores move.
